# ジロウタベタイ (Jirotabetai)

## Core Concept

ジロウタベタイ (Jirotabetai) is a dedicated portal site and vibrant online community designed specifically for fans of Jiro ramen. 

Inspired by the popular "サウナイキタイ" (Sauna Ikitai) platform for sauna enthusiasts, ジロウタベタイ aims to be the go-to resource for Jiro ramen lovers. It will provide a space for fans to discover new Jiro ramen shops, share their experiences and reviews, connect with fellow Jiro aficionados, and stay up-to-date on the latest news and information related to the world of Jiro ramen.

Whether you're a seasoned Jiro veteran or a curious newcomer, ジロウタベタイ will be your trusted companion on your Jiro ramen journey.

## Shop Information Features

ジロウタベタイ provides comprehensive information about Jiro ramen shops. Users can easily find details such as:

*   **Location:** Accurate addresses and map integration to help you find your way.
*   **Menu:** Information on available ramen types, toppings, and portion sizes.
*   **Opening Hours:** Up-to-date schedules, including any irregular closing days.
*   **Shop-Specific Rules:** Details on Jiro-specific ordering "calls" (e.g.,ニンニク入れますか？ - ninniku iremasuka?) and other shop customs.

Furthermore, ジロウタベタイ thrives on user-generated content (UGC). Users are encouraged to:

*   **Add New Shops:** If a Jiro ramen shop is missing, users can submit its information.
*   **Update Existing Information:** Help keep shop details accurate by suggesting edits or adding new information like temporary closures or menu changes.
*   **Share Photos:** Upload pictures of their Jiro ramen experiences.
*   **Write Reviews:** Share their thoughts, ratings, and tips for fellow Jiro fans.

## Jirolian Features (User Functionalities)

ジロウタベタイ offers a range of features for registered users (affectionately called "Jirolians") to personalize their experience and share their passion:

*   **User Registration & Profiles:**
    *   Simple registration process to join the community.
    *   Personalized user profiles where Jirolians can:
        *   Upload a profile picture.
        *   Declare their "Home Jiro" (ホームジロウ) - their favorite or most frequented Jiro ramen shop.
        *   Showcase their Jiro activity and contributions.

*   **Tracking Jiro Experiences:**
    *   **"Wanna Eat" (食べたい) List:** Jirolians can create and manage a list of Jiro shops they want to visit.
    *   **"Have Eaten" (食べた) Log:** Keep a record of all the Jiro ramen shops they've conquered, including dates and personal notes for each visit. This log can be used to track personal Jiro stats and memories.

*   **Posting & Community Interaction:**
    *   **Shop Reviews & Ratings:** Write detailed reviews, assign ratings to shops, and share tips about specific "calls," portion sizes, or queue times.
    *   **Update Shop Information:** Contribute to the accuracy of shop details by submitting updates (as mentioned in Shop Information Features, but initiated through their user account).
    *   **Photo Sharing:** Upload photos of their ramen, the shop exterior, or any other Jiro-related visuals.
    *   **(Future) Community Feed:** A potential feature where users can see a feed of recent reviews, photos, and updates from other Jirolians they follow or from shops they are interested in.

## Social & Community Engagement

ジロウタベタイ aims to foster a strong community among Jiro ramen enthusiasts. Key social features include:

*   **Follow Other Jirolians:**
    *   Users can follow other Jirolians whose reviews, photos, or Jiro expertise they appreciate.
    *   This allows users to curate a personalized feed of Jiro content (especially relevant for the future Community Feed feature) and stay updated on the activities of their favorite Jiro connoisseurs.

*   **Favorite Posts (イイネ！ - Iine!):**
    *   Users can "favorite" or "like" (using a term like "イイネ！" - Iine!, meaning "Good!") various types of content, such as:
        *   Shop reviews
        *   User-uploaded photos
        *   Helpful shop updates
    *   This feature helps highlight valuable content, provides positive feedback to contributors, and allows users to easily bookmark posts they find interesting or useful.
    *   Favorited posts could also be used to personalize recommendations or highlight popular content within the community.

## Our Goal: Connect, Share, Explore

In essence, ジロウタベタイ (Jirotabetai) strives to be the ultimate digital destination for everything Jiro ramen. It's a platform built by fans, for fans, with the core mission to help Jiro lovers:

*   **Connect:** Meet and interact with a passionate community of fellow Jirolians.
*   **Share:** Exchange experiences, tips, reviews, photos, and the latest shop information.
*   **Explore:** Discover new Jiro ramen shops, revisit old favorites, and deepen their appreciation for the unique world of Jiro ramen.

Join us in celebrating the culture and culinary adventure that is Jiro ramen!
